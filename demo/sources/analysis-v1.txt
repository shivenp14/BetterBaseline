1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE
This document outlines the statistical methodologies and operational analysis specifications for Study TX-2026-01. It details the primary analysis populations, handling of missing data, multiplicity adjustments, and formal hypothesis testing procedures.

2. ANALYSIS POPULATIONS
- Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population: All randomized participants, analyzed according to assigned treatment group.
- Per-Protocol (PP) Population: All ITT participants without major protocol deviations who completed minimum exposure criteria.
- Safety Population: All randomized participants who received at least one dose of study medication.

3. EFFICACY ENDPOINTS AND TIMING
3.1 Primary Efficacy Analysis
section: endpoint
Analyze the primary endpoint at week 24.
The primary comparison will evaluate the difference in proportion of responders between TS-101 and placebo using the Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel (CMH) test stratified by baseline disease severity category. Missing data at the primary assessment time point will be imputed using non-responder imputation (NRI) for the primary sensitivity benchmark.

3.2 Secondary Efficacy Analyses
Secondary binary endpoints will be tested hierarchically following confirmation of statistical significance at the primary endpoint. Continuous endpoints (change from baseline in disease scores) will be analyzed using a mixed-effects model for repeated measures (MMRM).

4. INTERIM ANALYSES
No formal interim efficacy stopping boundaries are planned. Safety analyses will be reviewed periodically by the independent Data Monitoring Committee.
