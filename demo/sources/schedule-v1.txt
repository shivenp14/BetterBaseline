1. VISIT SCHEDULE OVERVIEW
This document defines the visit windows, clinical assessments, drug administration milestones, and patient burden schedule for participating clinical sites.

Table 1: Clinical Site Visit Milestones
- Screening: Day -28 to Day -1
- Visit 1 (Baseline / Day 1): Randomization, initial IP dispensation, safety labs, baseline questionnaires
- Visit 2 (Week 4 +/- 3 days): Safety assessment, IP dispensation
- Visit 3 (Week 8 +/- 3 days): Safety assessment, biomarker collection
- Visit 4 (Week 12 +/- 5 days): Interim clinical assessment, IP dispensation
- Visit 5 (Week 16 +/- 5 days): Safety review, IP dispensation
- Visit 6 (Week 24 +/- 7 days): Primary efficacy milestone and comprehensive examination

2. PRIMARY VISIT PROCEDURES
section: endpoint
Schedule the endpoint assessment visit for week 24.
At this critical milestone visit (Visit 6), the following procedures must be completed:
- Investigator Global Assessment and composite disease activity index calculation
- Fasting safety laboratory draws and anti-drug antibody (ADA) titers
- Patient-reported outcome instruments (PROs) and functional questionnaire completion
- Investigational product accountability, reconciliation, and compliance check

3. PARTICIPANT SUPPORT AND TRAVEL
Sites must coordinate local participant travel assistance and transit vouchers for Visit 6 to minimize scheduling friction, given the extensive 4-hour clinical assessment duration required for primary endpoint evaluation.
