1. EXECUTIVE SYNOPSIS
This completed historical study evaluated early-generation biologic therapy across 120 adult patients. The dataset was formally locked in Q4 2024 and serves as an immutable comparative reference for future protocol design and effect-size power calculations.

2. STUDY OUTCOMES AND HISTORICAL TIMING
section: endpoint
Historical endpoint results were measured at week 24.
In the historical ITT cohort, 42.5% of active-arm patients achieved composite clinical remission at Week 24 compared to 18.3% of placebo patients (p = 0.004). Secondary radiographic progression and serum biomarker reductions also demonstrated peak separation at this predefined time milestone.
